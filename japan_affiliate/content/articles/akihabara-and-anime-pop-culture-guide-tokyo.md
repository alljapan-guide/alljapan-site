Many visitors come to Japan because of an anime, a manga or a game, and Tokyo is where that interest is easiest to follow. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) describes Akihabara as the centre of otaku culture, and its pop culture guide points to other districts for other tastes. This guide turns those pages into a plan. Where we add opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate. Shops, cafes and events change, and one JNTO source is a 2021 blog, so check each place's own page for current hours and details.

## Common mistakes

- **Trying three districts in one day.** Each one takes a long time, so choose one and add the others on another day.
- **Arriving without a list.** The shops are large and the stock is wide, so decide on a few things first.
- **Forgetting the luggage.** Figures and boxes are bulky. Our [luggage forwarding guide](japan-luggage-forwarding.html) explains how to send them to your hotel, and our [tax-free shopping guide](tax-free-shopping-in-japan-refund-method.html) explains how to claim tax back on purchases.
- **Not checking a cafe's rules.** Check the rules on each cafe's page.
- **Skipping the old part of the district.** The arcades near the tracks show the history JNTO describes.

## FAQ

### What is Akihabara known for?

JNTO says Akihabara, once all about gadgets and electronics, is now an even mix of electrical goods megastores, maid cafes and all things anime.

### How do I get to Akihabara Station?

JNTO says Akihabara Station is on the JR Yamanote and JR Sobu lines, and can also be reached by the Keihin Tohoku Line, the TX Tsukuba Express and the Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line.

### Where should I go for older anime series?

JNTO says to head to Nakano Broadway, a famous shopping complex with dozens of anime stores.

### What is Super Potato?

JNTO describes Super Potato as a famous video game store that epitomizes the otaku lifestyle, with retro games over three floors and an arcade with games from the 1980s and 1990s.

### Are there cafes with animals?

JNTO says Akihabara has animal cafes where you can hang out with owls, cats, otters, rabbits or hedgehogs, as well as anime cafes.
