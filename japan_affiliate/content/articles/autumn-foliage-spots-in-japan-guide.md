Autumn is a popular season for a trip to Japan, and the first question is where to go. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) publishes a short list of must-see autumn foliage spots, and this page follows it. We add how each place fits into a trip, which of our regional guides to read next, and a way to choose between them. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate. JNTO's page does not give peak colour dates, so use this guide to choose a place, then check the local tourism page for the current stage of the leaves.

## FAQ

### Which autumn foliage spots does JNTO recommend?

JNTO's page lists Goshikinuma in Fukushima, Showa Kinen Park in Tokyo, Nara Park in Nara, Kiyomizu-dera in Kyoto and Ritsurin Garden in Kagawa.

### Which spot is easiest from Tokyo?

Showa Kinen Park. JNTO says it is only 30 minutes away from central Tokyo by train.

### Is there an autumn illumination?

JNTO says Ritsurin Garden is famous for its autumn illumination event, usually held on the evenings of November and December.

### What else is there to do at Nara Park?

JNTO says the park houses Todaiji Temple, Kasuga Taisha Shrine, the Kofukuji National Treasure Hall and the Nara National Museum.

### When is the best time to see the leaves?

JNTO's page does not give dates, and the timing changes each year. Check the local tourism page for the current stage of the leaves.
