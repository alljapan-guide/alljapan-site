It is the night before you fly, or you have already landed, and you realise that you never bought travel insurance. The good news is that the official guidance says you are not too late. This guide uses the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) page on travel insurance to explain what is possible after you arrive, what to check before you buy and what to avoid. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate. It is general information, not advice about a specific policy.

## Questions to ask because you are buying late

Our view: buying after arrival raises a few questions that you would not face at home.

- **Does the policy start immediately?** Read the start date and any waiting period in the terms before you pay.
- **Are existing problems excluded?** JNTO notes that {{fact:ins_chronic}}.
- **Is anything that has already happened excluded?** Policies often exclude events that occurred before purchase, so do not assume that cover is retroactive.
- **How do you claim?** Find the claim steps and the emergency number before you pay, not afterwards.

{{warn|JNTO says that {{fact:ins_unpaid}}. If you have already been treated, keep every receipt and contact your insurer or card provider as soon as you can.}}

## FAQ

### Can I buy travel insurance after I arrive in Japan?

JNTO says it is possible to purchase insurance after you have arrived in Japan.

### Where can I buy it?

JNTO says the Japan Tourism Agency operates a dedicated website for safe travel, where you can easily purchase travel insurance.

### Does my credit card already cover me?

JNTO says some credit card companies offer travel insurance coverage, and asks you to ask your card provider for details.

### Is it mandatory?

JNTO says it is strongly recommended for all travelers. It does not describe it as an entry requirement.

### What if I am ill before I have a policy?

JNTO says the number to dial for an ambulance is 119, and the Japan Visitor Hotline is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
