## FAQ

### Can tourists buy a Suica or PASMO card in Japan?

The PASMO site lists notices of temporary suspension of sales of personalized and non-personalized Suica and PASMO cards, so check the current notice before you travel.

### What is Tourist PASMO?

PASMO says it is an IC card for international visitors, accepted across Japan, with no service charge, sold at Haneda Airport and Narita Airport. A notice dated April 17, 2026 said it was available soon.

### Can I use a phone instead of a card?

Apple says you can add a Suica, PASMO, ICOCA or TOICA card to Apple Wallet on an iPhone or Apple Watch.

### Does PASMO work on every train?

PASMO says it is not accepted on all train and bus networks in Japan.

### What if no card is available?

Buy paper tickets at the ticket machines, and add an IC card or a phone wallet card when you can.
