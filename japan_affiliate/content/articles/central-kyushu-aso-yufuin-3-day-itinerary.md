This guide follows the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) itinerary "Unwind in Central Kyushu". ## How to pace the three days

Our view: treat the first day as a travel-and-volcano day, since the journey from the airport or the station uses a large part of it. Keep the second day for Yufuin and the train, and the third for Hita and the return. If you have a fourth day, add Beppu rather than rushing the others.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: central Kyushu suits travellers who want hot springs, landscape and small towns, and who do not need a big city. It is a good choice in autumn and winter for the mist and the foliage. It is a weaker fit for a very short stay, since the transfers use a lot of time, and for anyone who prefers a car-free trip without bus changes. ## FAQ

### How many days does the itinerary take?

JNTO's Unwind in Central Kyushu has a recommended length of 3 days.

### How do I get to Mt. Aso from Tokyo?

JNTO says to fly from Haneda to Aso-Kumamoto Airport in 1 hour 45 minutes, then take a bus, the Higo Main Line and the Aso Crater Line bus, about 2 hours 30 minutes in all.

### What is the Yufuin no Mori train?

JNTO describes it as a limited express with wood interiors and buffet and salon cars, running from Yufuin to Hita in 55 minutes.

### When is the cormorant fishing season in Hita?

JNTO says the season for ukai lasts from May 20th to the end of October.

### Can I add Beppu?

JNTO lists Beppu as a popular hot springs resort east of Yufuin.
