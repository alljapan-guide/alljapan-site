When people say Chiba, they usually mean Disneyland. The prefecture across Tokyo Bay has much more for a family trip: an aquarium park, a mountain with cliffs and thousands of stone statues, and a farm where you can milk a cow. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) has a two-day plan that links them. This guide follows the JNTO itinerary "Explore Chiba's Theme Parks and Farms" and adds practical notes. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have one day, choose either the aquarium and the mountain, or the farm and the German village, but not both. If you travel with very young children, the farm and the park are easier than the mountain. If it rains, the aquarium and the hot spring work well, so keep the mountain for a clear day.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this trip suits families and groups who want a break from temples and museums, and who are happy to spend a day outdoors. It is less suited to travellers who want a quick sightseeing day in one place, since the stops are spread along the coast and need trains. It works well as a weekend trip. Pack a change of clothes for children, sun protection and a light rain jacket, because a day that mixes an aquarium, a mountain trail and a farm can bring several kinds of weather, and bring snacks for the train between stops.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Chiba's theme parks and farms?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 2 days.

### How do you get from Tokyo to Kamogawa Seaworld?

JNTO says it takes around 2 hours: the JR Sotobo Line Limited Express to Awa-Kamogawa Station, then a free shuttle bus that takes 10 minutes.

### What can you see at Kamogawa Seaworld?

JNTO says it is home to over 11,000 marine creatures across 800 different species, and the killer whale live show is a highlight.

### How big is Mother Farm?

JNTO says it is a sprawling 250-hectare farm-based amusement park where you can try milking cows, riding horses and making cheese and butter.

### What can you see from Mt. Nokogiri?

JNTO says the trails offer views of Tokyo Bay, Tokyo Skytree, the Miura Peninsula, Mt. Fuji and Izu Oshima.
