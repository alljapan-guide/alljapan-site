You do not always need to send luggage across the country. Sometimes you only need to leave a bag for the day, so that you can see a city without dragging it behind you. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) describes several ways to do that. This guide explains the options, what JNTO says about size, cost and payment, and when each one makes sense. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## When the lockers are full

JNTO warns that {{fact:hf_lockers}}. In busy locations the lockers fill up very quickly, so plan an alternative before you arrive.

Our view: at big stations at the weekend, arrive at the lockers early, or go straight to a staffed storage counter. A busy morning is the worst time to be looking.

## Planning around storage

Our view: storage works best when you plan the day around it. Leave the bags, see the main sights near the station, and collect them with enough time for your train. If your next hotel is in another city, consider sending the bags ahead instead, and keep only a small day bag. If you need a base near a big station, you can compare stays on {{aff:trip_hotels|Trip.com}}, and our hotel guides for [Tokyo](where-to-stay-in-tokyo.html), [Kyoto](where-to-stay-in-kyoto.html) and [Osaka](where-to-stay-in-osaka.html) explain which areas are convenient.

## FAQ

### Are there coin lockers in Japanese train stations?

JNTO says coin lockers of various sizes are available at most stations and tourist facilities.

### How much does a coin locker cost?

JNTO says that {{fact:hfx_locker_cost}}, and that {{fact:hfx_locker_range}}.

### Can I pay with an IC card?

JNTO says some lockers accept Suica or Pasmo payment if you do not have coins.

### Where can I store a large suitcase?

JNTO says to use a luggage storage service for odd-sized luggage, and that airports like Narita, Haneda and Kansai usually offer luggage storage.

### What if the lockers are full?

JNTO warns that lockers in very busy locations fill up very quickly, so research where else to store your bags before you go.
