## FAQ

### How do I get from Tokyo to Fukuoka?

JNTO says it takes about five hours by shinkansen from Tokyo Station to Hakata Station, or about one hour and 45 minutes by air from Haneda Airport to Fukuoka Airport.

### How far is the airport from the city centre?

JNTO says the airport is only six minutes away from Hakata Station by subway.

### What is Fukuoka famous for eating?

JNTO's pages highlight tonkotsu ramen, sushi and seafood, yakitori, motsunabe hot pot, mizutaki and baked curry.

### When is the Hakata Dontaku Festival?

JNTO says it is held every year in May during Golden Week, drawing 2 to 3 million people.

### What is Dazaifu Tenmangu?

JNTO describes it as a shrine dedicated to the scholar Michizane Sugawara, home to over 6,000 plum trees.
