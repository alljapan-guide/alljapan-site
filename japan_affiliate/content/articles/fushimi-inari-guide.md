Fushimi Inari Taisha is the Kyoto shrine famous for its path of vermilion torii gates, and it is one of the easiest major sights to reach by train. The official Kyoto tourism association gives clear access advice, including one bus route it tells visitors to avoid.

This guide follows the Kyoto City Tourism Association's own pages for access and for the shrine. Where we add opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get to Fushimi Inari from Kyoto Station?

The official access page says about 9 minutes on the JR Nara Line, getting off at JR Inari Station.

### Which trains stop at JR Inari Station?

According to the official page, all rapid trains and local trains on the JR Nara Line stop there.

### Do Keihan limited express trains stop at Fushimi Inari Station?

No. The official page says limited express trains do not stop at Fushimi Inari Station, so choose another train type.

### Which bus should I avoid?

The official page recommends trying to avoid the City Bus South No. 5, because its travel time is expected to vary by a large margin.

### How many torii gates are there?

The Kyoto tourism association says over 5000 vibrant orange torii gates wind through the hills behind the shrine.
