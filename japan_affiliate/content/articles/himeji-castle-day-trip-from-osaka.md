## FAQ

### How do I get to Himeji Castle from Himeji Station?

JNTO says the castle is one kilometer down Otemae-dori street from the north exit, a 15-to-20-minute walk or five-minute bus ride.

### Is Himeji Castle free to enter?

JNTO says Otemon Gate gives access to the admission-free outer area called San-no-maru, and the inner castle is the paid part.

### Can I climb to the top of the keep?

Yes. JNTO says it is possible to climb the keep to the top floor, where there is a small shrine and viewpoints.

### Is Himeji busy in cherry blossom season?

JNTO says the castle is at its most crowded then, and tickets to enter the main keep may be limited.

### What is Kokoen?

JNTO describes it as nine separate walled gardens in Edo-period styles, next to the castle.
