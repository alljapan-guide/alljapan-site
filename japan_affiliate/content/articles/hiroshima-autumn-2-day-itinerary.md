## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have one day, choose Miyajima in the morning and the Peace Memorial Park in the late afternoon, and skip the garden and castle. If you want a relaxed autumn trip, add a night on the island so you can see the shrine after the day-trippers leave. If you travel in the peak foliage weeks, book accommodation first, then plan sights around it.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this itinerary suits travellers who want one city with both a famous natural setting and a serious history, and who are happy to walk. It works well as a break between Kyoto or Osaka and Kyushu, because the Shinkansen links all three. The two days balance each other: Miyajima is scenic and busy, while the city is reflective and quieter. If you only have a short stay, do not rush the memorial park, since it is better to see fewer places and give it time. Check the foliage reports and your tide times before you finalise the day order.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Hiroshima in autumn?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 2 days, one for Miyajima and one for Hiroshima city.

### When do the maple leaves turn red on Miyajima?

JNTO says Momijidani Park has about seven hundred maple trees that turn brilliant shades of red from mid- to late-November.

### How do you get to Miyajima?

JNTO says to take the JR Sanyo Line to Miyajimaguchi Station, then board the ferry bound for Miyajima, a journey of 50 minutes.

### What should you eat in Hiroshima?

JNTO suggests okonomiyaki, a hearty hybrid of fried noodles and savory pancake, and mentions Okonomimura Village with around 25 restaurants.

### Can you stay overnight on Miyajima?

JNTO says there are several Japanese inns and hotels on Miyajima Island, most near the port area on the north side.
