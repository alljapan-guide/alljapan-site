## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Tokyo to Hiroshima?

JNTO says you can reach the city from Tokyo by shinkansen in around four hours, or about 90 minutes from Osaka.

### How do I get to Miyajima from Hiroshima?

JNTO says to take the Sanyo Line from Hiroshima Station to Miyajimaguchi Station in around 30 minutes, then a ferry of around 10 minutes.

### What is Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki?

JNTO says it layers its ingredients rather than mixing them together, and is served on a bed of griddle-cooked noodles.

### Can anyone join the lantern ceremony?

JNTO says that anyone can join the lantern ceremony held on August 6.

### Is there a tram in Hiroshima?

Yes. JNTO says Hiroshima has around 300 trams in service on the Hiroden tram service, more than any other city in Japan.
