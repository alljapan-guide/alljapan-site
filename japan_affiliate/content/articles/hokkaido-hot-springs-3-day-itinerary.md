## How to pace the three days

Our view: keep the first day light, since the journey from Tokyo is already long. Use day 2 for the lake and the volcano in good weather, and keep day 3 for the harbour city's morning market, which opens early. Pack layers, since the climate differs from Tokyo, and check the season before you book.

## What to carry and what to check

Our view: bring layers, waterproof shoes and a small towel for the hot spring stops, and check the opening hours of the market, the ropeway and the theme park before you go. The weather and the season change what is open and how long the journeys take, so keep the second day flexible.

## Who this route suits

Our view: this route suits travellers who want hot springs, scenery and seafood in one trip, and who can fly north for a few days. It is a good choice for a second trip to Japan, or for travellers who enjoy trains and a slower pace. It is less suited to a very short holiday, or to anyone who prefers big-city sightseeing.

## FAQ

### How many days does the itinerary take?

JNTO's Hot Springs and Nature in Hokkaido has a recommended length of 3 days.

### How do I get from Tokyo to Noboribetsu?

JNTO says to fly from Haneda to New Chitose Airport, then take trains and a bus to Noboribetsu Onsen, about 2 hours 35 minutes of flying plus transfers.

### What is Hell Valley?

JNTO says it is where hot water gushes out at Noboribetsu Onsen, and you can hike the landscape then relax in a naturally heated bath.

### Is Mt. Usu active?

JNTO says Mt. Usu is an active volcano that last erupted in 2000.

### What is the Hakodate morning market like?

JNTO says it opens at 5 a.m. from May to December or 6 a.m. from January to April, with crab, squid and shellfish.
