## How to pace the two days

Our view: start day 1 early so you have time for the zoo and the snow park before dark, and keep day 2 for the village and the evening in the city. Allow extra time for travel in snow, and keep a buffer for flight changes. If you only have one day, choose either Asahikawa or Sapporo, not both.

## What to pack and prepare

Our view: bring a warm waterproof coat, insulated boots with good grip, gloves and a hat, and a spare layer for the train. Phones and cameras lose battery quickly in the cold, so keep a power bank in an inside pocket. Plan indoor stops between outdoor ones, such as a cafe near the zoo or a station building in Sapporo, so the day does not feel like one long stretch in the snow. Our [travel insurance guide](japan-travel-insurance.html) explains why cover is worth checking for winter activities.

## Getting around in winter

Our view: allow extra time for every transfer, since snow and cold can slow trains and flights, and keep your first and last days flexible. Carry tickets and reservations on your phone and on paper, and check the weather and the festival dates a day before you travel.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this itinerary suits travellers who want a winter experience with animals, festivals and snow, and who are comfortable in cold weather. It works for families and for first-time winter visitors. It is a weaker choice for travellers who dislike the cold, or who want a mild city break.

## FAQ

### How many days does the itinerary take?

JNTO's A Winter Trip to Hokkaido has a recommended length of 2 days.

### How do I get to Asahikawa from Tokyo?

JNTO says there are direct flights from Tokyo Haneda to Asahikawa Airport, taking 1 hour 45 minutes.

### When are the snow festivals?

JNTO says the Asahikawa Winter Festival is held every February, and the Sapporo Snow Festival is held in February every year.

### What can I see at Asahiyama Zoo?

JNTO says you can watch penguins fly through water from a tunnel below and meet polar bears from a glass dome.

### When does the Sapporo White Illumination begin?

JNTO says it begins in late November.
