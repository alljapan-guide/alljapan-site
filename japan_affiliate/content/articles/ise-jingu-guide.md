1. **Start early at the outer shrine** and keep to the left.
2. **Visit the main hall, then the first betsugu.**
3. **Travel to the inner shrine** and keep to the right.

5. **Add Futami or other betsugu** if you have time.

## FAQ

### Which shrine should I visit first at Ise?

JNTO says to visit the outer shrine first, then the inner one, the same order the imperial family follows.

### Which side of the path should I walk on?

JNTO says to keep to the left at the outer shrine, and to the right at the inner shrine.

### How far apart are the two main shrines?

JNTO says they are 6 km apart, so you need to plan your route in advance.

### How do I pray at Ise Jingu?

JNTO says to bow deeply at the torii gate, clap twice and pray, then bow again, after purifying your hands and mouth at the chozuya.

### What are the basic manners?

JNTO says smoking is prohibited in the shrine area, you should speak softly, switch your phone to silent mode, avoid exposing too much skin and take off your hat in front of the god.
