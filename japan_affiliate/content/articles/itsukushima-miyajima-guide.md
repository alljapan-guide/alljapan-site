## A simple plan for a day

1. **Morning:** Take the train from Hiroshima to Miyajimaguchi and the ferry to the island.
2. **Shrine:** Visit Itsukushima Shrine and see the gate at the tide of the day.
3. **Afternoon:** Choose Daishoin, Senjokaku or the pagoda.
4. **Optional:** Take the ropeway up Mt. Misen if the weather is clear.
5. **Return:** Allow time for the ferry and the train.

## FAQ

### How do I get to Miyajima from Hiroshima?

JNTO says it takes around 30 minutes from Hiroshima Station to Miyajimaguchi Station on the Sanyo Line, then a ferry of around 10 minutes.

### Why does the torii stand in the sea?

JNTO says the island was once considered so sacred that it was separated from the shrine, so the entrance gate is constructed offshore.

### Can I walk to the torii?

JNTO says you can walk out to the large torii gate at low tide.

### Is there a fee for the island?

JNTO says a small visitor tax is included in ferry tickets, with exemptions for local residents, commuters and young children. Check the official Miyajima website for details.

### What else is there to see?

JNTO lists Daishoin Temple, Daiganji Temple, Senjokaku, the five-story pagoda and a ropeway up Mt. Misen.
