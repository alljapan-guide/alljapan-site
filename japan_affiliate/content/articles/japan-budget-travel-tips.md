This guide summarises that page and adds our own view, labelled "Our view", so you can tell sourced tips from opinion.

We do not quote prices here, because they change. Check current prices with each provider before you decide.

## How to turn the tips into a plan

Our view: pick the two or three tips that fit your trip and ignore the rest. A traveller who stays in one region might choose a regional pass, a business hotel and lunch sets. A traveller covering long distances might compare a pass, an overnight bus and a budget flight on the same route. A traveller who loves food might spend on dinner and save on rooms. Write your daily budget as a simple range for each category, then compare it with current prices before you book, since the prices in this guide's sources change. Keep a small buffer for transport you did not plan, because a missed train or a closed sight can change a day's cost.

## Where not to cut costs

Our view: do not save on insurance. A single unplanned medical bill can erase every saving above. Our [travel insurance guide](japan-travel-insurance.html) summarises the JNTO checklist. Also avoid cutting so many rooms and meals that you waste time hunting for food late in the day.

## FAQ

### Is Japan expensive for tourists?

It can be, but JNTO publishes tips for saving on transport, accommodation, food, culture and shopping.

### Should I buy a rail pass to save money?

JNTO says that for extensive travel throughout Japan the JR Rail Pass may suit, while regional passes may offer more value if you stay local. Compare it with individual tickets first.

### What are cheap places to stay?

JNTO lists business hotels, capsule hotels and boutique hostels, and says some traditional ryokans can be found at the cheaper end of the spectrum.

### How can I save on food?

JNTO suggests lunch-time specials, all-you-can-eat or all-you-can-drink deals, and cooking at home if your accommodation has a kitchen.

### Are there free things to do?

Yes. JNTO says many temples, shrines and parks are free of charge.
