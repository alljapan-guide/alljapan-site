## FAQ

### What is an ekiben?

JNTO describes it as a boxed meal made for on-the-go eating, sold at train stations for journeys.

### How much does an ekiben cost?

JNTO says that {{fact:ek_price}}.

### Can I eat ekiben on any train?

JNTO says eating and drinking is acceptable on long-haul express trains such as the shinkansen, but short-distance local trains are not the appropriate place for a meal.

### Are there vegetarian ekiben?

JNTO says larger stations such as Tokyo Station have several vegetarian and vegan alternatives, including a Vegetable Bento.

### Are there gluten-free ekiben?

JNTO says gluten-free options are a rarity in Japan, and most ekiben involve a dish cooked with soy sauce.
