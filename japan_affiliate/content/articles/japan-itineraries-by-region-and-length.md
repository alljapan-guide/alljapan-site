There are many ways to plan a trip to Japan, and the hardest part is often choosing where to start. This page is a map to the itineraries on this site. Each one follows a Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) route and adds practical notes, and you can pick one by trip length, by region or by what you like. Where we add opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

{{photo:1|A bamboo grove in Arashiyama, Kyoto}}

## How to choose in two minutes

```diagram
type: flow
caption: A quick way to choose an itinerary
steps: How many days do you have? | First trip: start with Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka | Second trip: choose a region you have not seen | Pick one main interest: culture, hot springs, nature or food | Add a day trip only if the transfer is short
```

Our view: the biggest planning mistake is trying to see too much. Most first-time visitors enjoy a trip more when they stay in each base for at least two nights, and use day trips for the rest.

## If you have a weekend, or two to three days

Our view: this is the length where a small region is best, because you can stay one night in a hot spring town or a temple lodging.

- **Near Tokyo.** [Hakone](hakone-trip-from-tokyo.html) is a two-night plan with art and views, and JNTO says that {{fact:hkn_len}}. [Nikko](nikko-trip-from-tokyo.html) and the [Fuji Five Lakes](fuji-five-lakes-3-day-itinerary.html) suit nature lovers. [Yamanashi hot springs](yamanashi-hot-springs-2-day-itinerary.html), [Izu Oshima](izu-oshima-island-2-day-itinerary.html) and [Chiba for families](chiba-theme-parks-farms-2-day-itinerary.html) are quieter options.
- **Snow and winter.** [Nagano's snow monkeys](nagano-snow-monkeys-hot-springs-2-day-itinerary.html), a [Niigata winter weekend](niigata-winter-weekend-itinerary.html) and [Hokkaido in winter](hokkaido-winter-2-day-itinerary.html) are the main choices.
- **Around Kyoto and Osaka.** The [Kansai three-day plan](kansai-3-day-itinerary-kyoto-nara-osaka.html) links Kyoto, Nara and Osaka, and JNTO says that {{fact:kg_len}}. [Osaka in three days](osaka-3-day-itinerary.html) adds day trips, and [Amanohashidate](amanohashidate-trip-from-kyoto.html) is a coastal escape from Kyoto.
- **Sacred places.** [Koyasan](koyasan-mt-koya-2-day-itinerary.html), [Ise](ise-food-and-culture-2-day-itinerary.html) and [Kumano Kodo](kumano-kodo-pilgrimage-itinerary.html) suit travellers who want a temple stay or a pilgrimage walk.
- **Hiroshima and the Inland Sea.** [Hiroshima in autumn](hiroshima-autumn-2-day-itinerary.html) and the [Shimanami Kaido cycling route](shimanami-kaido-cycling-itinerary.html) suit a trip west of Osaka.
- **Tokyo experiences.** [Cultural classes in Tokyo](tokyo-cultural-experiences-classes.html) work well as a half-day add-on.
- **Tohoku.** [Sendai and Matsushima](sendai-matsushima-akiu-3-day-itinerary.html), [Aizu](aizu-samurai-2-day-itinerary.html), [Shirakami and Juniko](shirakami-juniko-tohoku-2-day-itinerary.html) and the [summer festivals](tohoku-summer-festivals-itinerary.html) cover the north.

- **Five days.** [Takayama to Kyoto](hida-takayama-to-kyoto-5-day-itinerary.html) adds mountain villages and Kanazawa, and JNTO says that {{fact:hdk_len}}.
- **Six days.** The [Shikoku itinerary](shikoku-6-day-itinerary.html) covers castles, onsen and festivals, and JNTO says that {{fact:shk_len}}.
- **Four days.** [Oita hot springs](oita-hot-springs-beppu-yufuin-4-day-itinerary.html) are a good base for a slow trip.
- **Three days in Kyushu.** [Central Kyushu](central-kyushu-aso-yufuin-3-day-itinerary.html), [Nagasaki and Saga](nagasaki-saga-ceramics-3-day-itinerary.html), [Kyushu culture](kyushu-culture-3-day-itinerary.html) and [Kumamoto to Kagoshima](kumamoto-to-kagoshima-by-rail-itinerary.html) are all plans of about three days.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Japan?

JNTO's classic Golden Route is a two-week itinerary, and shorter plans of one to three days fit a weekend or a stopover.

### What is the best first itinerary for Japan?

Our view is the seven-day Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka plan, and JNTO's Golden Route if you have two weeks.

### Which itinerary is best for hot springs?

The Oita, Yamanashi, Akiu and Hakone plans on this site are built around hot spring towns.

### What is a good short trip from Tokyo?

Kamakura, Yokohama, Kawagoe and Mt. Takao are all one-day plans, and Hakone and Nikko are two-day plans.

### Which itinerary suits winter?

The Nagano snow monkeys, Niigata winter weekend and Hokkaido winter plans are built around the snow season.
