## What kinds of tours exist

Not every guided tour is a temple walk. JNTO notes that {{fact:g_niche}}. Our view: a themed tour can be a better first choice than a general one if you already know what you care about, for example food, pop culture or nature.

### When a guide is worth the cost

Our view: a guide is most valuable where the context is the point. Temples, shrines and old districts reward explanation, while a shopping day does not. If your time in a city is short, a guide also removes the cost of getting lost. JNTO frames the benefit this way: {{fact:g_learn}}. We think that holds best when you have a few hours and a specific place in mind.

## Questions to ask before you book

A short exchange with the agency or guide saves most problems. Our view: send these questions in writing and keep the replies.

- Will a National Government Licensed Guide Interpreter lead the tour, and can you confirm that in writing?
- Which language will the tour be in, and does the guide cover the language you need from the list above?
- Where do we meet, how long does the tour last, and what is included?
- Is the route fixed, or can it change to match what we want to see?
- What are the cancellation and rebooking terms if the weather or a closure changes the plan?

Our view: a guide who answers clearly and quickly before you pay is usually a good sign. If the answers are vague, treat that as information and look at another option.

## FAQ

### What is a National Government Licensed Guide Interpreter?

JNTO describes them as guides who have passed the national examinations. Their services are available in 10 languages.

### Which languages are available?

JNTO lists English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean and Thai.

### How do I hire a licensed guide?

JNTO says you can request one when you book your trip or book tours through a travel agency, or go through a national or regional organization.

### Is a licensed guide guaranteed on every tour?

No. JNTO says to always check and confirm that a Licensed Guide Interpreter will be conducting the tour, as it is not always a guarantee.

### Are guides employees of a company?

JNTO says that, generally, they are independent professionals.
