Japan is easy to visit, but a few local rules catch first-time visitors out: where you may smoke, what outdoor drinking looks like, how tattoos are treated at baths and pools, and how seriously drug laws are enforced. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) has a page on exactly these points, and this guide walks through it.

Where we add advice, we label it "Our view", so you can tell JNTO's guidance from our opinion. JNTO also notes that {{fact:law_ignorance}}, which is a good reason to read the rules before you travel.

## How to apply these rules day to day

A few small habits cover most of what JNTO describes. Our view: treat each rule as a question to answer once, then forget about it.

- **On arrival:** decide where your passport will be kept each day, and keep the copy on your phone.
- **At a restaurant or bar:** if you smoke, ask where the designated area is before you order, not after the meal.
- **In a park or at a festival:** if you plan a picnic, bring a bag for your rubbish and take it with you when you leave.
- **At a bath, pool or gym:** check the written rules on the venue's website, and if the rules are unclear, ask before you pay.
- **With medicines:** if you are unsure about an item, ask your embassy or the airline before you pack.

## What these rules mean for your trip

None of the points above should stop you from visiting. They are a reminder to plan a few small things.

- **Smoking:** find designated areas, and do not smoke while walking.
- **Outdoor drinking:** stay quiet and take your litter with you.
- **Tattoos:** check each bath, pool or gym in advance.
- **Drugs:** follow the law strictly, including for items prescribed at home.
- **Documents:** carry your passport.

## FAQ

### Do I need to carry my passport in Japan?

JNTO says you should carry your passport with you at all times as a form of identification.

### Can I smoke on the street in Japan?

JNTO says smoking is not allowed in most restaurants and bars except in designated areas, and the smoking rule is strictly enforced and can result in a fine. Find a smoking area.

### Can I drink alcohol outdoors?

JNTO says drinking outside does not violate any laws and is part of the culture, but loud and boisterous behavior is not, and you should take your litter home.

### Can I use a hot spring with a tattoo?

JNTO says many baths, pools, hot spring resorts and gyms prohibit entry to anyone with tattoos, even small ones, while some establishments will ask you to cover them, and it advises researching in advance.

### Is cannabis treated differently in Japan?

No. JNTO says cannabis is treated in the same way as other drugs, and possession of a small amount can lead to a prison sentence.
