Keep the middle of the day for sights with fixed hours, such as museums, and check each one's own page. Eat lunch at a restaurant whose hours you have confirmed. This rhythm avoids the two most common problems, which are arriving at a closed door and queuing in the busiest hours.

## FAQ

### What time do shops open in Japan?

JNTO says shops are generally open from 10:00am to 8:00pm every day in urban areas, and in rural areas they often close earlier.

### Are there 24-hour stores?

Yes. JNTO says many convenience stores, as well as some chain restaurants and discount stores, operate 24 hours a day, even on national holidays.

### When do trains stop running?

JNTO says most trains run from around 5:00am to midnight. Use a timetable app to check last trains.

### When are shrines and temples open?

JNTO says they are generally open year-round, with grounds often accessible from morning until late afternoon, and the shrine office has specific hours.

### Which holidays should I avoid?

JNTO names New Year, Golden Week and Obon as the three major holiday seasons when domestic travel peaks and many businesses may close or operate on reduced hours.
