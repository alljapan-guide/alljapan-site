## Credit card cover and buying after arrival

JNTO says {{fact:ins_card}}. Our view: cover through a card can be a good starting point, but confirm the details with your card provider, as the page itself asks, and compare them against the checklist above.

If you forget to buy before departure, JNTO says {{fact:ins_after_arrival}}. It points to a Japan Tourism Agency website for safe travel where you can buy a policy online.

## FAQ

### Is travel insurance mandatory for Japan?

JNTO's page says it is strongly recommended for all travelers. It does not describe it as an entry requirement.

### Can I buy travel insurance after I arrive in Japan?

Yes. JNTO says you can sign up once you arrive, and points to a Japan Tourism Agency website for safe travel.

### Does my credit card already cover me?

Possibly. JNTO says some credit card companies offer travel insurance coverage, and asks you to confirm the details with your card provider before leaving.

### What number do I call for an ambulance?

JNTO says the number to dial for an ambulance is 119.

### Is there a help line for visitors?

Yes. The Japan Visitor Hotline is run by JNTO and is open around the clock, with support in English, Chinese and Korean.
