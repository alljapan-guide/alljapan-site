Whether you need a visa for Japan depends on your passport, and the answer can change. This guide summarises what the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) publishes for tourists, shows you what to check, and points you to the government pages that decide the question.

It is general information, not legal advice. JNTO says as much on its own page, and so do we: {{fact:visa_change}}. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so you can tell sourced information from editorial advice.

## The one thing every visitor needs

JNTO's entry page begins with the basics: {{fact:visa_passport}}. Everything else, including whether you need a visa, follows from the passport you hold.

Our view: check the validity of your passport before you do anything else, including booking flights. A passport problem found at the airport costs far more than one found at home.

## FAQ

### Do I need a visa to visit Japan as a tourist?

It depends on your passport. JNTO says passport holders of some countries and regions do not need a visa if the stay is 90 days or less and for tourism only, and it links to the official list.

### How long can I stay in Japan as a tourist?

JNTO says most short-term stays allow up to 90 days, and some nationalities may be granted 15, 30, or 180 days.

### How long does a tourist visa take?

JNTO says processing times vary but generally take around five business days. Apply early anyway.

### Is the Visit Japan Website mandatory?

JNTO says registration is not required, but advance registration makes procedures like tax-free shopping more convenient.

### Can I work on a tourist visa?

No. JNTO says that on a visa for tourists you cannot engage in paid work, enroll in long-term educational programs or apply for residency.
