## A simple plan for one day and one night

Our view: arrive by midday and put the garden first, since light and crowds are best early and late. Spend the afternoon in the historical centre, with a stop for a craft or a local sweet. Keep the evening for dinner, and leave the following morning for the market before you board the shinkansen. If you add a second night, use it for a hot spring town rather than a second round of sights in the city.

This pace follows the way JNTO groups the area: the garden, the historical centre and the market each get their own entry, which suggests treating each as a separate visit rather than squeezing them into a single walk.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Tokyo to Kanazawa?

JNTO says it takes about 2 hours 30 minutes on the Hokuriku Shinkansen.

### How do I get to Kanazawa from Kyoto or Osaka?

JNTO says to take the JR Limited Express (Thunderbird) to Tsuruga, then transfer to the Hokuriku Shinkansen, for a total of about 2 hours.

### What are the main sights in Kanazawa?

JNTO lists Kenrokuen Garden, the Higashi Chaya District, Omicho Market and Kanazawa Castle Park among the trending attractions.

### What crafts is Ishikawa known for?

JNTO highlights Kutani porcelain, Wajima lacquerware, Kaga yuzen silk dyeing and Kanazawa lacquerware.

### When is the best time for cherry blossoms in Kanazawa?

JNTO says you can view over 400 blossoming cherry trees in Kenrokuen Garden in spring.
