Kansai is the region around Kyoto, Osaka and Nara, and it is the easiest place in Japan to build a trip from short train rides. This page is a planning hub for the region. It does not repeat each guide; it helps you choose a base, decide which day trips fit, and pick a longer trip if you have more days. Every route comes from the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO), and where we add opinion we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to use Kansai as a base

JNTO says that {{fact:ot_kansai}}. That sentence is the whole logic of the region. Most visitors choose one base, leave bags there, and take the rest as day trips.

```diagram
type: flow
caption: A simple way to choose your Kansai base
steps: Count your nights in Kansai | Two to three nights: choose Kyoto or Osaka, not both | Four or more nights: split them between Kyoto and Osaka | Add one day trip for each full day you can spare | Book the base first, then fit day trips around it
```

Our view: Kyoto suits temples and quiet evenings, and Osaka suits food, late nights and easy trains in every direction. If you only have one base, Osaka reaches more day trips, while Kyoto is calmer once the day trippers leave. Our hotel guides for [Kyoto](where-to-stay-in-kyoto.html) and [Osaka](where-to-stay-in-osaka.html) explain which areas work for a short stay.

Our view: do not try to see all three in a single day. Nara is the one that fits best as a half-day add-on to a Kyoto or Osaka stay, because the train is short and the park is compact.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Kansai?

JNTO's Kansai Trio plan is 3 days for Kyoto, Nara and Osaka. Add a day for each extra destination such as Kobe or Himeji.

### Should I stay in Kyoto or Osaka?

Stay in Kyoto for temples and a quieter evening, and in Osaka for food and easy trains. If you have only one base, Osaka reaches more day trips.

### Is Nara a day trip or a half day?

JNTO says Nara is just over 30 minutes by train from Kyoto and Osaka, so it works as a half day or a full day depending on how many sights you choose.

### What is the shortest day trip from Osaka?

Kobe is the shortest of the trips in this guide. JNTO gives 25 minutes on the JR Kobe Line from Osaka Station to Sannomiya Station.

### Can I visit Koyasan without staying overnight?

JNTO recommends 2 days for its Koyasan itinerary, which includes a temple stay. A day visit is possible in principle, but check the travel times in our Koyasan guide first.
