Tokyo is the best base in Japan for day trips, because a dozen very different places are within two hours by train. The hard part is not finding them; it is choosing, because a single day cannot cover a temple town, a harbour city and a mountain at once. This page is a planning hub for the Tokyo area and the regions just beyond it. It does not repeat each guide; it compares the trips by travel time, shows which ones need a night and helps you build a plan. Every route comes from the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO), and where we add opinion we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## FAQ

### What is the best day trip from Tokyo?

It depends on your interest. JNTO gives 35 minutes to Kawagoe, 40 minutes to Yokohama and about 1 hour to Kamakura, so those three are the easiest first trips.

### Can I visit Hakone or Nikko in one day?

JNTO describes both as 2-day itineraries. A day visit is possible, but you will see less, so stay a night if you can.

### Which day trip suits a first visit?

Kamakura is the classic first choice, and Yokohama is the easiest. Both are one-day JNTO plans.

### Which trips work in winter?

JNTO has 2-day winter plans for Nagano and Niigata. Check the operator's page for snow conditions before you go.

### Do I need a rail pass?

Not necessarily. Most of these trips use standard fares, so compare the fares with a pass before you buy.
