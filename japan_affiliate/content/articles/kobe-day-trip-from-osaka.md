## Who this day suits

Our view: Kobe suits travellers who already have Osaka's food and neon covered and want a calmer, greener contrast. It works well for couples, for anyone who likes harbours and old foreign houses, and for a day when the weather is clear enough for the ropeway views. It is a weaker choice for a very short Osaka stay, since the train takes only 25 minutes but the day still needs several hours on foot, and the route is best done without a rush.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Osaka to Kobe?

JNTO says it takes 25 minutes on the JR Kobe Line from Osaka Station to Sannomiya Station.

### Is Kobe worth a day trip?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 1 day, so it works as a full day out from Osaka.

### What is Kobe beef?

JNTO says it comes from locally bred and raised Tajima cattle and must meet a strict set of standards to carry the Kobe stamp.

### Does the Japan Rail Pass cover the trip from Tokyo?

JNTO says it is covered if you take a Hikari train from Tokyo to Shin-Kobe.

### What else is near Kobe?

JNTO points to Mt. Rokko and Arima Onsen, one of Japan's oldest hot spring towns.
