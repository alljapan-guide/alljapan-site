## Plan around three areas

JNTO divides the city into areas, and a first visit works best if you pick a few rather than try everything. The three below are the ones that most first-timers want.

```diagram
type: cards
caption: Three Kyoto areas for a first visit, from the JNTO area pages
items: Gion and Higashiyama=Temples, old townscapes, the Philosopher's Path and geisha districts | Central Kyoto=Nijo Castle, the Imperial Palace park, Shimogamo-jinja and the Nishijin textile district | Beyond the centre=Ohara, Takao and northern Kyoto by bus from Kyoto Station
```

Our view: choose one area per half day. Moving between areas costs more time than it looks on a map, and the city's best moments come when you are not rushing.

## FAQ

### How many days do I need in Kyoto?

JNTO says to expect at least a day for Gion and Higashiyama alone, with two days and an evening recommended for that area.

### What is the best time of day to visit temples?

JNTO recommends visiting shrines at less busy times, such as early in the morning or late at night when they are lit up.

### Can I take photos of geiko and maiko?

JNTO asks visitors not to stop them for photos or touch their clothes, and suggests booking a maiko experience instead.

### Is smoking allowed on Kyoto's streets?

JNTO says street smoking is strictly prohibited, with penalties outside designated areas in the city centre.

### How do I check how crowded a place is?

JNTO points to the Kyoto Travel Congestion Forecast map, which includes live cameras.
