## Common mistakes

Our view: these are the slips the JNTO facts point to.

- **Assuming the season is open for months.** It runs from early July to early September.
- **Arriving in Obon week without a plan.** JNTO says trails are most crowded then.
- **Skipping the official site.** JNTO tells you to check it for the latest information.
- **Leaving a hut booking late.** Huts fill quickly in peak season.
- **Treating a day climb as normal.** JNTO says it is possible but not recommended.
- **Dressing for the bottom.** Summit temperatures are far lower than at the base.

## FAQ

### When is the Mt. Fuji climbing season?

JNTO says the climbing season starts in early July and ends in early September, and that peak season lasts from late July to late August.

### Which trail is best for beginners?

JNTO describes the Yoshida Trail as the most popular and accessible route, with many facilities and a separate descent route, ideal for beginners and sunrise hikes but often crowded.

### Do I need a permit to climb Mt. Fuji?

JNTO says no special permit is required, but a mandatory hiking fee is requested. Check the official climbing website for the current rules.

### Can I climb Mt. Fuji in one day?

JNTO says it is possible but not recommended, and that most hikers prefer two days with an overnight stay.

### Do I need a guide?

JNTO says guides are not essential, but for a stress-free climb you can consider joining a guided tour, which can handle transport, guides, meals and hut reservations.
