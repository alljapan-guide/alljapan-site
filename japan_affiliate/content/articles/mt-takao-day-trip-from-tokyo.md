Mt. Takao is the hike that fits into a Tokyo trip: a mountain with a temple, a monkey park and a hot spring at the bottom, reached by train from Shinjuku. This guide follows the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) itinerary "Mt. Takao—A Mountain of Surprises". Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## Three ways to do the day

| Version | How it works | Suits | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hike up and down | Trail No. 1 from Takaosanguchi Station | Walkers who want the whole climb | Start early and plan the descent before dark |
| Ride up, walk down | Cable car or chairlift up, trail down | Most visitors; keeps the views and spares the climb | Schedules vary by season |
| Ride both ways | Cable car or chairlift in both directions | A short day or limited mobility | The lifts go only halfway, so the summit is still a walk |

Our view: the middle version is the safest first plan, because you decide how much of the trail to walk once you see the weather and your own energy.

## Who this day suits

Our view: Mt. Takao suits visitors who want a taste of nature without a long journey, and who are comfortable on a trail for a couple of hours. It is a good choice for a clear day, for autumn colours and for travellers who want to see Mt. Fuji from a summit. It is less suited to a rainy day, when the views and the paths suffer, or to travellers with limited mobility, although the cable car and chairlift help.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Shinjuku to Mt. Takao?

JNTO says 1 hour 10 minutes from Tokyo, with direct Keio trains from Shinjuku Station, and GO TOKYO says about 55 minutes from Shinjuku Station on the Semi Limited Express. Plan on about an hour and check the timetable.

### How long is the hike?

JNTO says trail No. 1 takes about 100 minutes to the top, and a cable car and chairlift go halfway up. GO TOKYO says the summit is about 40 minutes from the upper cable car station.

### Can I see Mt. Fuji from Mt. Takao?

JNTO says a spectacular view of Mt. Fuji awaits you at the summit.

### Are there monkeys on Mt. Takao?

JNTO says the Mt. Takao Monkey Park is home to over 90 monkeys.

### Is there a hot spring nearby?

JNTO says Keio Takaosan Onsen Gokurakuyu is next to Takaosanguchi Station, with indoor and outdoor baths, massage services and a restaurant.

### Can the trails or the cable car be closed?

GO TOKYO says trails can close after typhoons, heavy rain or bear sightings, and that bus and cable car schedules vary by season. Check before you go.
