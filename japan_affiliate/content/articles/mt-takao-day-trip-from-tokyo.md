## How to pace the day

Our view: leave early so you reach the summit in good light and have time for the onsen before the last trains. Carry water and a light layer, and eat at one of the food stalls JNTO mentions on the trails rather than carrying a full lunch. If you want a gentler day, ride up and walk down, which keeps the views and spares your knees.

## Who this day suits

Our view: Mt. Takao suits visitors who want a taste of nature without a long journey, and who are comfortable on a trail for a couple of hours. It is a good choice for a clear day, for autumn colours and for travellers who want to see Mt. Fuji from a summit. It is less suited to a rainy day, when the views and the paths suffer, or to travellers with limited mobility, although the cable car and chairlift help.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get from Shinjuku to Mt. Takao?

JNTO says it takes 1 hour 10 minutes, with direct Keio trains to Takaosanguchi Station from Shinjuku Station.

### How long is the hike?

JNTO says trail No. 1 takes about 100 minutes to the top, and a cable car and chairlift go halfway up.

### Can I see Mt. Fuji from Mt. Takao?

JNTO says a spectacular view of Mt. Fuji awaits you at the summit.

### Are there monkeys on Mt. Takao?

JNTO says the Mt. Takao Monkey Park is home to over 90 monkeys.

### Is there a hot spring nearby?

JNTO says Keio Takaosan Onsen Gokurakuyu is next to Takaosanguchi Station, with indoor and outdoor baths, massage services and a restaurant.
