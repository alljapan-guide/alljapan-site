## How to adapt the day

Our view: the five stops are not equal. If you want a relaxed day, choose three: the castle, one museum and the station for dinner. If you arrive from Tokyo in the morning, store luggage at the station and start with the castle; if you arrive in the afternoon, start with the Toyota museum and end at the castle area only if it is still open.

## FAQ

### Is one day enough for Nagoya?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 1 day, which covers the castle, the museums and the station area.

### How long does it take to get from Tokyo to Nagoya?

JNTO says it takes 1 hour 40 minutes on the Tokaido-Sanyo Shinkansen from Tokyo or Shinagawa stations.

### How long does it take to get from Osaka to Nagoya?

JNTO says it takes 50 minutes on the Tokaido-Sanyo Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka Station.

### What should you eat in Nagoya?

JNTO suggests miso-katsu pork cutlets covered in red bean hatcho miso paste, kishimen flat noodles and ogura toast.

### When is the best time to visit Meijo Park?

JNTO says early May is a particularly good time to visit when the wisterias are in full bloom, and that the area around the castle is also a popular cherry blossom spot in spring.
