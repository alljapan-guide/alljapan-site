## FAQ

### How long does it take to get to Nara from Kyoto?

The official Nara guide lists about 41 minutes by JR Rapid Express from Kyoto Station to Nara Station, and about 34 minutes by Kintetsu Limited Express to Kintetsu-Nara Station.

### How long does it take from Osaka?

The same guide lists about 52 minutes by JR Rapid Express from Osaka Station, and about 34 minutes by Kintetsu Express from Osaka-Namba.

### Is Nara worth a day trip?

Our view: yes, if you want temples, the Great Buddha and the deer in one walkable area. The official guide says you can expect fewer crowds than Kyoto.

### Which station should I use?

Kintetsu-Nara Station is a short walk from the park according to the official guide. From JR Nara Station, the guide says the walk along Sanjo-dori Street takes roughly 20 minutes.

### Is it safe to feed the deer?

The official guide says special crackers are sold around the park, and warns you to keep other food and leaflets out of sight because the deer may mistake them for food.
