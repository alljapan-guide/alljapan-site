If you want the hot spring trip that Japan is famous for, Oita prefecture on the island of Kyushu is one of the most concentrated places to do it. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) has a four-day plan that links two very different hot spring towns with a quiet peninsula of old temples. This guide follows the JNTO itinerary "The Hot Springs of Oita" and adds practical notes. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have two nights, keep Yufuin and Beppu and drop Kunisaki. If you want a long soak, choose a ryokan with its own bath and stay a night instead of squeezing the baths into a day trip. If you travel in a busy season, book your accommodation first and plan the sightseeing around it.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Oita hot springs?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 4 days, but two nights cover Yufuin and Beppu.

### How do you get from Tokyo to Yufuin?

JNTO says it takes 2 hours 40 minutes: a flight from Haneda to Oita Airport takes 1 hour 35 minutes, then an express bus takes 55 minutes to Yufuin Station Bus Center.

### What are the Hells of Beppu?

JNTO says they are hot springs that spew water of up to 150 degrees Celsius, with minerals turning them hues from cobalt blue to blood red.

### What is jigokumushi food?

JNTO says it is seafood and vegetables cooked over hot springs in bamboo steamers.

### Can day visitors use the baths in Yufuin?

JNTO says the outdoor baths at Sansuikan and Musouen are highly recommended and that both inns allow non-staying guests to use their facilities for a fee.
