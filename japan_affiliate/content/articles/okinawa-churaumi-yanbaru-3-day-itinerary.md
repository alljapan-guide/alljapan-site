## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have two days, keep day 1 and day 3 and replace the waterfall with extra time at the aquarium. If you want a beach day, add a night at the coast rather than trying to fit swimming into a day of bus transfers. If you travel with children, the aquarium and the zoo make the trip easy, and the night hike is better kept for older children.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need in northern Okinawa?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 3 days, which covers the aquarium, the forest and the mangroves.

### How do you get to Churaumi Aquarium from Naha Airport?

JNTO says to take the Okinawa Airport Shuttle bus (rapid) from Naha Airport to the Kinen Koen Mae (Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium) bus stop, which takes 2 hours.

### What are the opening hours of the aquarium?

JNTO says it is open from 8:30 a.m. until 6.30 p.m. Check the official site before you go, since hours can change.

### What can you see at Churaumi Aquarium?

JNTO says it is home to around 10,000 fish of about 680 species, with whale sharks and manta rays in the main tank.

### Can you canoe in the mangroves?

JNTO says Higashimura Fureai Hirugi Park has mangroves that you can view from a promenade or from a canoe.
