Osaka works well as a base for three days, because the best day trips in western Japan all leave from it. This guide builds a three-day plan around Osaka: one day in the city and two day trips that you choose between. It uses the Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) pages we have summarised in our other guides, and it adds our own opinion, labelled "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## FAQ

### Is three days enough for Osaka and day trips?

Yes for one day in the city and two day trips. JNTO says Kyoto, Nara, Kobe and Wakayama are each about a one-hour train ride from Osaka's central hubs.

### Should I do Kyoto or Nara first?

Our view: Kyoto is the longer day on foot, so pick it when you are fresh. Nara is an easier second option.

### How long does the Kobe trip take?

JNTO says it takes 25 minutes on the JR Kobe Line from Osaka Station to Sannomiya Station.

### How do I get to Himeji Castle from the station?

JNTO says it is one kilometer down Otemae-dori street from the north exit of Himeji Station, a 15-to-20-minute walk or five-minute bus ride.

### Where should I stay?

Our view: near a major Osaka station. Our Osaka hotel guide explains the areas.
