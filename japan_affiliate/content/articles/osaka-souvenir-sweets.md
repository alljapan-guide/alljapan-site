## FAQ

### What are the best souvenir sweets from Osaka?

JNTO picks mitarashi mochi, okonomiyaki-flavoured prawn crackers called Osaka Okonomiyaki Ebi-ten, and konpeito sugar candy.

### How long does mitarashi mochi last?

JNTO says the expiry date is about three days, and recommends buying it on the day you leave Osaka.

### Where can I buy Osaka Okonomiyaki Ebi-ten?

JNTO says the only place to buy it is at Shin-Osaka Station.

### What is konpeito?

JNTO describes it as a traditional sugar candy brought to Japan by Portuguese traders in the early 16th century, now sold in colourful tins.

### Are these gifts easy to carry?

JNTO notes that mitarashi mochi is not ideal to pack in a suitcase because of the sticky sauce. Konpeito is sold in tins and is easier to carry.
