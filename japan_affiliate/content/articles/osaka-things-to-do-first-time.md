## FAQ

### How many days do I need in Osaka?

JNTO says the castle alone can take anywhere from an hour to a full day. Our view: two days is enough for the castle, Dotonbori and a few meals out.

### How do I get to Osaka Castle?

JNTO says to take the JR Osaka Loop Line from Osaka or Umeda Station to Morinomiya Station, next to the park.

### How do I get to Dotonbori?

JNTO says to take the Midosuji Line from Osaka or Umeda Station to Shinsaibashi Station, or continue to Namba Station.

### What food should I try?

JNTO's Osaka pages highlight takoyaki and okonomiyaki, along with crab restaurants and riverside stalls in Dotonbori.

### Can I visit Kyoto or Nara from Osaka?

JNTO says Kyoto, Nara, Kobe and Wakayama are each about a one-hour train ride from Osaka's central hubs.
