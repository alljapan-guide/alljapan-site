## FAQ

### Can I send my luggage from the airport to my hotel in Japan?

JNTO says Narita, Haneda and Kansai International Airport all offer luggage delivery services where you can drop your luggage off to be delivered to your hotel.

### How do I send luggage from my hotel to the airport?

JNTO says the options all require advance booking at least a day ahead, and luggage is normally delivered to a designated counter to be picked up before you check in.

### Is same-day delivery possible?

JNTO says some services offer same-day delivery if you send before 11am.

### What cannot be sent?

JNTO says valuables, fragile items, fresh products and liquids, and dangerous and hazardous materials cannot be accepted.

### What if my bag is lost or damaged?

JNTO says compensation for damaged or lost items is provided, the amount may vary depending on the carrier, and a tracking service is available.
