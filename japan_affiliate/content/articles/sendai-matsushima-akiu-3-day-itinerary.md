Sendai is the easy first step into northern Japan. It is a big, comfortable city that sits one bullet-train ride from Tokyo, and it opens onto a famous bay and a quiet hot spring valley. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) links the three in a three-day plan. This guide follows the JNTO itinerary "Matsushima, Akiu Onsen and Sendai" and adds practical notes. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have two days, keep Matsushima and Akiu and treat Sendai as an evening. If you travel with children, a cruise and the falls walk are the easy wins; the gorge and craft village suit a slower day. If you travel in winter or after heavy rain, check bus services and trail conditions before you set out, since mountain roads can change quickly.

Our [Hokkaido winter itinerary](hokkaido-winter-2-day-itinerary.html) shows another northern trip, and our [Japan weather guide](japan-weather-by-season.html) explains the seasons.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this itinerary suits travellers who have seen Tokyo and Kyoto and want a calmer region with the same kind of sights: shrines, a famous view, hot springs and local craft. It is less suited to a first trip with only a week in Japan, because the three areas lie in different directions from Sendai Station. If you have a rail pass, check whether the Tohoku route is covered before you decide, and keep the first night in Sendai flexible so you can respond to the weather. Pack for temperature swings, since a bay cruise, a gorge walk and an evening bath feel very different from each other.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for Sendai and Matsushima?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 3 days, covering Sendai, Matsushima Bay and Akiu Onsen.

### How do you get from Tokyo to Sendai?

JNTO says Sendai is best reached by bullet train on the JR Tohoku Shinkansen, and trains depart up to seven times an hour.

### How long is a Matsushima cruise?

JNTO says cruise courses in the bay last from 25 to 70 minutes.

### What is Matsushima famous for?

JNTO says Matsushima Bay is dotted with over 260 small islands and inspired the poet Matsuo Basho.

### Is Akiu Onsen worth a night?

JNTO says you can book an overnight stay at one of the many ryokan or guesthouses in Akiu, and the town has a waterfall, a gorge and a craft village.
