Most visitors see Japan from a train window. The Shimanami Kaido lets you see it from a bicycle, over a chain of bridges that cross the Seto Inland Sea. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) has a plan that treats the route as a one or two day trip. This guide follows the JNTO itinerary "Cycling the Shimanami Kaido" and adds practical notes. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have a day, ride a section rather than the whole route, for example Onomichi to Ikuchijima, and return by public transport. If you want the full route, choose a two-day version with a night on one of the islands, and book accommodation in advance, since choices are limited. If you do not want to ride at all, you can still visit Onomichi and its temples as a half-day trip.

For a nearby trip, our [Itsukushima guide](itsukushima-miyajima-guide.html) covers the famous shrine on Miyajima, and our [Shikoku itinerary](shikoku-6-day-itinerary.html) shows where the route ends.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: the Shimanami Kaido suits travellers who want an active day that is also a sightseeing day, and who are comfortable on a bicycle in traffic-free lanes. It is a better choice than a bus tour if you like stopping when you see something interesting. Pack light, wear sun protection and bring a small repair kit if the rental shop offers one. If you are unsure about your fitness, ride the first section and decide at Ikuchijima whether to continue, because the highway bus back means you are never stuck.

## FAQ

### How long is the Shimanami Kaido?

JNTO describes it as a scenic 70 km-long bike route from Honshu to Shikoku that crosses over six islands.

### How long does it take to cycle?

JNTO says experienced cyclists can complete the 70-kilometer route in about four hours, and a leisurely ride takes about 10 hours.

### How do you get to Onomichi from Tokyo?

JNTO says it takes 4 hours 30 minutes, with the Shinkansen to Fukuyama Station and the JR Sanyo Line to Onomichi Station.

### Can you rent bicycles in Onomichi?

JNTO says Onomichi has many shops that offer bicycle maintenance and rentals.

### How do you get back after you finish?

JNTO says you can drop your bike off in Imabari and return to Onomichi by highway bus, which takes about 1.5 hours.
