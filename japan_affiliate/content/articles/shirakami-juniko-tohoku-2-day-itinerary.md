## How to pace the two days

Our view: arrive early on day 1 so you have time for the lakes before dark, and keep the evening for the bath. On day 2, take the coast train slowly and stop at Senjojiki for the walk. If you have only one day, choose either the lakes or the coast, not both.

## What to pack and what to check

Our view: bring waterproof shoes for the lake trails, a warm layer for the coast, and a small towel for the hot spring. Check the bus times from Juniko Station before you leave the train, since local buses run less often than city services, and keep your luggage light so the transfers are easy.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this itinerary suits travellers who have seen the big cities and want a slower, scenic stretch. It is a good choice for walkers, photographers and rail fans, and a weaker choice for a first short visit to Japan or for anyone who needs to move quickly. Pack layers and walking shoes.

## FAQ

### How many days does the itinerary take?

JNTO's Scenic World Heritage in Tohoku has a recommended length of 2 days.

### How do I get there from Tokyo?

JNTO says it takes 4 hours 45 minutes: the Akita Shinkansen to Akita, the Limited Express Tsugaru to Higashi-Noshiro, and the Resort Shirakami train to Juniko Station.

### Why is it called Juniko, or twelve lakes?

JNTO says there are in fact 33 lakes, but only 12 could be seen from the collapsed mountain.

### What is Furofushi Onsen known for?

JNTO says it is famous for an outdoor bath that protrudes into the Sea of Japan.

### What is on the way back to Tokyo?

JNTO suggests Kakunodate, a 17th century samurai town famed for its cherry blossoms.
