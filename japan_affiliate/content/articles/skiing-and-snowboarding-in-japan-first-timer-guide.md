## Mistakes to avoid

Our view: these are the choices that cost ski trips the most.

- **Booking the peak weeks late.** The holidays and early February are the busiest, so book rooms and lessons early.
- **Choosing a resort by name.** Match the resort to your level and your language needs.
- **Ignoring the transfer time.** The bus leg can take longer than the flight.
- **Skipping the insurance check.** Many policies exclude dangerous activities, so confirm that skiing is covered.
- **Packing everything.** Rentals are available at every resort, so bring only what you cannot rent.

## FAQ

### When is the ski season in Japan?

JNTO says it generally begins in December and lasts until April, with dates depending on each resort. The peak is January and February.

### Is Niseko or Hakuba better for a first trip?

Niseko has the most English options and is beginner friendly, according to JNTO. Hakuba has 10 resorts, though most are not connected to each other.

### Can I ski as a day trip from Tokyo?

Yes. JNTO names Gala Yuzawa, which has a gondola connected to the train station, and Kagura, reached by shinkansen to Jomo Kogen Station.

### Do I need to bring my own gear?

No. JNTO says every ski resort in Japan has rental outlets, and renting a set is usually more economical.

### Are snowboarders welcome?

Yes. JNTO says almost all resorts allow snowboarders, but check in advance for any off-limits areas.
