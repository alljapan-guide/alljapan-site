## Common questions before you buy a card

Our view: most visitors have the same three worries. First, will the card work with a foreign payment card? Apple says that an eligible payment card in Wallet is needed, so check that yours is accepted when you add the card, and try a small top-up at home. Second, what if the phone runs out of battery? Express Mode is for tapping at gates, and a flat phone cannot tap, so carry a power bank and have a paper-ticket plan. Third, what if you change phones? Apple describes how to move the card, and our [guide to adding or transferring a card](transfer-suica-pasmo-card-to-iphone-apple-wallet.html) explains the steps.

## A pre-flight test

Our view: a ten-minute test avoids a bad first day.

1. Check your model against the list above.
2. Check that two-factor authentication is on for your Apple Account.
3. Add a card and a small amount at home.
4. Turn on Express Mode for the card.
5. Confirm that the phone works with low battery, and carry a power bank.

## FAQ

### Which iPhone do I need for Suica?

Apple says that for Suica you need an iPhone 7 or later, and for the other cards an iPhone 8 or later.

### Can I use an Apple Watch?

Apple says you need an Apple Watch Series 3 or later, and for Suica a Series 2 or later that was purchased in Japan.

### Do I need two-factor authentication?

Apple says you need an Apple Account that you used to sign in on your device with two-factor authentication.

### Can children use Apple Pay for transit?

Apple says Apple Pay is not available for children under 13, and the age varies by country or region.

### Can I use more than one card with Express Mode?

Apple says Express Mode can only be turned on for one card at a time.
