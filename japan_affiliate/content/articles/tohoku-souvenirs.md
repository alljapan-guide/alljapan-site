## FAQ

### What are the best souvenirs from Tohoku?

JNTO's story picks Namahage Manju and morokoshi from Akita, Kingyo Nebuta from Aomori, sweets from Koiwai Farm in Iwate, and Hanagori from Yamagata.

### What is Namahage Manju?

JNTO describes it as a Japanese sweet in the shape of the face of the Namahage demon, with red bean paste wrapped in a soft castle cake.

### Where can I buy Kingyo Nebuta?

JNTO says it is available at Aomori Airport, Aomori Station and several large stations throughout the prefecture.

### Is Koiwai Farm open all year?

JNTO says the opening hours vary depending on the time of year and weather, and asks visitors to check the official website.

### How old is the JNTO story?

It was published on 24 December 2019, so check each shop for current products and hours.
