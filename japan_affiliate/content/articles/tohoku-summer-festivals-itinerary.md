Early August is the best time to see Tohoku. Three of Japan's most colourful summer festivals fall within a few days of each other, and they can be linked in a single trip. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) has a plan for it. This guide follows the JNTO itinerary "Touring Through Tohoku's Lively Summer Festival Circuit" and adds practical notes. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate.

## How to adapt the plan

Our view: if you only have three days, choose two festivals and give each a full evening. The Aomori and Akita festivals are the ones that overlap most, so check the exact dates before you decide. If you do not like crowds, visit the museums and the onsen on a different date and see only one festival.

## Who this trip suits

Our view: this trip suits travellers who like festivals, lanterns and local food, and who are comfortable with crowds and heat. It suits a second visit to Japan, when you have already seen the main cities. It is less suited to travellers who need quiet or fixed plans, since festival dates and crowds drive everything. Pack for heat and for sudden rain, wear comfortable shoes for standing, and carry a small towel and water. Because trains and hotels fill early in festival week, decide your route first, reserve the Shinkansen seats as soon as they are on sale, and keep one flexible evening in case a performance is delayed or cancelled by the weather. If you travel with children, choose a viewing spot with room to move and plan a quiet meal before the crowds arrive. Keep your phone charged for maps and train times, and agree on a meeting point with your group in case you are separated in the crowd.

## FAQ

### How many days do you need for the Tohoku summer festivals?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 3-5 days.

### When is the Aomori Nebuta Festival?

JNTO says it is held between August 2 to 7.

### When is the Akita Kanto Festival?

JNTO says it runs from August 3 to 6.

### When is the Sendai Tanabata Festival?

JNTO says it runs from August 6 to 8.

### How do you get from Tokyo to Aomori?

JNTO says it takes 3 hours 10 minutes, and trains on the Tohoku Shinkansen to Shin-Aomori Station depart hourly.
