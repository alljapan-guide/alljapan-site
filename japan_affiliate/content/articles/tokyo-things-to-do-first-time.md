## A simple three-day plan

1. **Day 1:** Meiji Jingu, then Harajuku and Shibuya.
2. **Day 2:** Asakusa and Sensoji, then the Sumida River.
3. **Day 3:** Akihabara, then a food-focused evening.

## FAQ

### What are the top things to do in Tokyo for a first-timer?

JNTO lists eating across the city, Akihabara, Harajuku and Shibuya, and Sensoji with a Sumida River ferry.

### Which airport serves Tokyo?

JNTO says Tokyo is served by two international airports, Haneda and Narita.

### What is Akihabara known for?

JNTO describes it as the home of technology and anime culture, with the latest electronics, games and gadgets.

### What can I do in Tokyo in spring?

JNTO says you can picnic under the blossoms at parks such as Shinjuku Gyoen, Yoyogi, Ueno or Inokashira, but should check each park's rules.

### Is there something to do in winter?

JNTO says illuminations appear across the city, ice-skating rinks open and crowds visit Sensoji and Meiji Jingu at New Year.
