## FAQ

### How do I get from Tokyo to Kyoto?

JNTO says Kyoto is easily accessible from Tokyo via the Tokaido-Sanyo Shinkansen, and JR Central says the trip should definitely be by Shinkansen.

### Can I book the shinkansen online from abroad?

JR Central says you can book from one year before departure, both inside and outside of Japan, for up to 6 people at once.

### Do I need to collect a paper ticket?

JR Central says that with a QR ticket you simply display it and go directly to the ticket gate, with no need to stop at the station counter.

### Does the Japan Rail Pass cover Nozomi trains?

No. The pass page says NOZOMI and MIZUHO trains are not covered and need a separate ticket.

### Where can I leave my luggage in Kyoto?

JNTO says there are coin lockers and luggage storage facilities around rail stations.
