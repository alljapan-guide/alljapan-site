## How to shorten the day

Our view: if you are short on time, keep the three anchors that JNTO lists as highlights: the market, the tower and the shopping district. Drop Ryogoku first, since it needs a separate museum decision, and skip the Skytree deck on a cloudy day. If you have energy at the end, spend the extra time in Shibuya rather than adding another sight, because the evening is where the route pays off.

## Who this day suits

Our view: this route suits first-time visitors who want a sample of every side of Tokyo in a single day, and who are happy with a lot of short train rides. It is a poor fit for travellers who want a slow day with a single sight, in which case our [one-day cultural itinerary](tokyo-one-day-cultural-itinerary.html) is a better choice. If the weather is wet, move the Skytree later and keep the indoor stops first.

## FAQ

### Is one day enough for Tokyo?

JNTO's itinerary has a recommended length of 1 day, so it covers a themed sample of the city rather than all of it.

### How do I get to Tsukiji from Tokyo Station?

JNTO says to take the JR Keiyo line to Hatchobori Station and the Hibiya Line to Tsukiji Station, then walk 1 minute to the outer market.

### Is the Tsukiji fish market still there?

JNTO says the Tsukiji Fish Market moved to Toyosu on October 11, 2018, but the outer market remains a lively business district.

### How high is the Skytree observation deck?

JNTO says the observation deck is 450 meters off the ground.

### What is Akihabara known for?

JNTO says it has transformed into a center for anime, video games and J-pop.
