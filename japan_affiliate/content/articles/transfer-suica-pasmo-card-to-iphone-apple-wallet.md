Setting up a transit card on your iPhone before you fly means that you can tap in and out of Japanese stations from the first train. Apple publishes the steps in its support page on adding a transit card in Japan. This guide follows that page, adds the points that matter to a visitor and keeps the unofficial advice labelled. Where we give opinion, we label it "Our view", so sourced information and editorial advice stay separate. Software changes, so check the Apple page for your version of iOS.

```diagram

steps: Open Wallet and tap Add | Choose Transit Card | Choose Suica, PASMO or another card | Tap Add New Card | Choose an amount to put on the card and confirm
```

Our view: choose a first amount that covers your first days, and top up later when you know how much you use. You do not need a large balance at the start. Do the setup on home Wi-Fi before the flight, so that a slow connection at the airport does not hold you up.

## What to check before you fly

Our view: a short list avoids surprises.

1. Your device is on Apple's list of supported models.
2. You are signed in with two-factor authentication.
3. You have an eligible payment card in Wallet to add money.
4. You have set up the card, and tried the Express Mode setting.
5. You know where you will top up, and what you will do if the phone battery runs out.

## FAQ

### How do I add a Suica to my iPhone?

Apple says to open the Wallet app, tap Add, choose Transit Card, choose the card, tap Add New Card and choose an amount to put on the card.

### Can I move my physical Suica to my iPhone?

Apple says you can transfer your physical transit card, its current balance and commuter plan to Apple Wallet.

### Will my plastic card still work after the transfer?

Apple says that after the transfer, your physical card will not work.

### Can I have the card on my iPhone and my Apple Watch?

Apple says you can have a transit card on only one device at a time, but you can move it between devices.

### What is Express Mode?

Apple says it lets you pay for transit rides and purchases with just a tap, and it can be turned on for only one card at a time.
