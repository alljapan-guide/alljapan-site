## Questions to ask before you book

Our view: a short list of questions avoids most problems.

1. **Is the guide a National Government Licensed Guide Interpreter?** JNTO says to always confirm.
2. **Which languages does the guide speak?** Check against the list above.
3. **What is included?** Ask whether transport, entrance fees and meals are in the price.
4. **How long is the tour, and where does it start?** Get the meeting point in writing.
5. **What is the cancellation policy?** Read it before you pay.
6. **Is the route suitable for your group?** Mention children, older relatives or mobility needs.

{{tip|Send the same short message to two or three guides, with your dates and interests, and compare the answers. The tone and detail of the reply tell you a lot about the tour.}}

## A sample request message

Our view: a clear first message gets a clear answer. Include your travel dates, the city, the number of people and their ages, the languages you need, your main interests, any mobility needs and your budget range. Ask for the guide's licence status, the meeting point and the cancellation terms, and ask whether transport and entrance fees are included. Keep the reply in writing and confirm the details a few days before the tour, so that the guide and the meeting point are not a surprise on the day.

## FAQ

### Where can I hire a licensed guide in Japan?

JNTO lists national organizations such as the Japan Federation of Certified Guides and regional ones such as the Kansai Interpreter and Guide Association.

### How do I request a licensed guide?

JNTO says you can request one when you book your trip or tours through a travel agency, or make a request through a national organization.

### What languages do licensed guides speak?

JNTO says their services are available in 10 different languages, including English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Korean and Thai.

### How do I know the guide is licensed?

JNTO says to always check and confirm that a Licensed Guide Interpreter will be conducting the tour, as it is not always a guarantee.

### Are there guides for specific interests?

JNTO says some guides have set up their own tours in niches from food, nightlife and pop culture to heritage and nature.
