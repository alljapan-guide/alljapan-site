## FAQ

### Where should first-time visitors stay in Osaka?

Our view is Namba for food and the airport line, or Umeda for easy day trips. JNTO describes each area so you can choose.

### Is Osaka a good base for Kyoto and Nara?

JNTO says Osaka is an excellent base for exploring the greater Kansai region, and that each area is a one-hour train ride from the central hubs.

### How do I get from Kansai Airport to Namba?

JNTO says to take the Nankai line to Namba Station, or the Nankai or JR lines to Tennoji or Namba.

### Should I stay near Universal Studios Japan?

JNTO lists a day trip to the park as something not to miss in the bay area. Our view is that a central base is enough unless the park is the point of your trip.

### Which Osaka station do I arrive at by bullet train?

JNTO says the bullet train stops at Shin-Osaka. Osaka Station is the main hub for local trains, four kilometers away.
