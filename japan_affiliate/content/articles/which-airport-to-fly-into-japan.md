Where you land shapes the first day of your trip. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) describes the main international airports and what each is close to. This guide uses those pages to help you choose, and adds our own view, labelled "Our view", so you can tell sourced information from opinion.

The short version: pick the airport closest to the first city you want to sleep in, and consider flying into one airport and out of another.

## Open-jaw trips: land in one place, leave from another

Our view: if you plan to travel between Tokyo and Kyoto or Osaka, consider flying into one airport and out of another so you do not have to return to your starting point. The train between Tokyo and Kyoto takes {{fact:tokyo_kyoto_130}} on the bullet train, according to JR Central, which makes a one-way route workable. ## A quick way to decide

Our view: answer three questions before you compare fares.

- **Where is your first night?** Choose the airport closest to that city, using the JNTO descriptions above.
- **How much luggage do you have?** A shorter transfer matters more with heavy bags. Our [luggage forwarding guide](japan-luggage-forwarding.html) explains how to send bags ahead to your hotel.
- **What time do you land?** A late arrival leaves little room for a long transfer, so check the last trains before you book.

## FAQ

### Which airport is closest to Tokyo?

JNTO says that Haneda Airport lets visitors be in Tokyo within half an hour of leaving the terminal. Narita is an hour-long express train ride from Tokyo.

### Which airport is best for Kyoto?

JNTO says Kansai International Airport serves the western area of Japan including Kyoto, and that it is around 75 minutes from Kyoto Station.

### Can I visit Japan without going to Tokyo?

Yes. JNTO says it is now possible to visit Japan without stepping foot in Tokyo at all.

### Can I arrive by boat?

JNTO says it is also possible to reach Japan by sea, with ferries between cities such as Busan in South Korea and Japanese cities.

### How do I choose between airports?

JNTO advises researching the airports that will offer you your most convenient path into Japan. Compare fares, transfer times and your first city.
