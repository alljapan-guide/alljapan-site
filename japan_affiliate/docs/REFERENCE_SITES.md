# Reference sites and what they teach (Oct 2026)
Format: blog / guide media with affiliate links. We do NOT sell anything; articles only send readers to partners.

## English-language, Japan-focused (our primary model)
| Site | Scale (third-party/self-reported) | Money model | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| japan-guide.com | Similarweb: ~2M visits over 3 months (Aug 2026) | ads + affiliate (not verified) | Huge info base; old authority, hard to beat head-on |
| Japan Cheapo / Tokyo Cheapo | >850k monthly reach combined (self-reported) | affiliate (tours, hotels) + sponsored posts | "Cheap/practical" angle + deep city coverage |
| The Navigatio | ~3M readers/yr claimed; ~$5k/mo (2023) | ads 45%, hotels 35%, rail pass 15% | Solo-founder scale proves the model; community (Facebook group) drove traffic |
| GoToTravel.tw | ~700k monthly readers, ~$6.7k/mo affiliate (vendor case study) | Travelpayouts | Treat as marketing figure |
Sources: Similarweb pages for japan-guide.com / japan.travel; nichepursuits.com Navigatio interview; travelpayouts.com case study; japancheapo.com.

## Japanese-language affiliate blogs (Plan B model)
- Self-reported cases: ~10k PV -> ~6k-64k JPY/mo depending on luck with high-price offers; ~100k PV -> ~80k-100k JPY/mo; some niche blogs reach 540k-1.7M PV (unverified, camping gear etc.).
- Rule of thumb quoted: affiliate-only ~5-10 JPY/PV, AdSense ~0.3-1 JPY/PV (single-blog claim).
- Lesson: narrow niches with high-ticket or comparison intent beat broad "general" blogs.

## What this means for our design
1. Money comes from **decision-stage travel content** (hotels/areas, passes, eSIM, tours). Products (snacks, knives, skincare) are low-commission (Amazon ~3-5% older rates; Bokksu/Dokodemo ~3-12%) and crowded, so they are a **secondary cluster** (priority <= 8).
2. A "Japan products" niche works only if narrow (e.g. a single category with real depth) - evidence: a Gundam spin-off outperformed a broad Japan blog.
3. Do not start by competing with japan-guide.com on breadth. Win long-tail "decision" queries (X vs Y, where to stay, is Z worth it) in a few clusters, then expand.
4. Realistic: most affiliates earn very little (Tokyo Weekender); several months before revenue.
